                    Forecast settings

                    If shifts up to a Friday are paid the next Thursday, enter 6. Use 0 if a paycheck covers the 2 weeks right before it.

                    How the forecast works
                    1. Pay: the hours of the shifts you enter × your pay per hour × your take-home %. The app learns the take-home % by comparing past shifts with the paychecks they turned into. With no shifts for a payday, it uses the average of your last 4 paychecks. You can also type the pay for any payday yourself.
                    2. Out: bills due before the next payday, semester fund amounts, your usual spending, and any pay advances that will be taken back.
                    3. Left: what's left now, plus pay, minus what goes out, carried from one payday to the next.
                    4. Could go negative: what's left would drop below $0 if this paycheck turned out as low as your lowest recent one.
